## Product Principles

1. **Proof over claims.** Show live sites, real stacks and measured facts; no skill percentages, no inflated tool lists.
2. **Thirty-second clarity.** Name, role, work, experience, contact must be findable at a glance by a non-technical reader.
3. **Depth on demand.** Engineering detail is one interaction away for those who want it, never in the way of those who don't.
4. **Honest labels.** Every project says what it is: product, client, proposal, concept or course project.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Sound is optional with a visible toggle and never carries information alone. Every interaction works by keyboard and touch; reduced-motion users get a calm, complete page.
