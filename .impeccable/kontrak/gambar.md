# Surface brief: /gambar/ (comparison round, variant B)

Scope: the whole portfolio as one page, English, for HR recruiters screening a full-stack developer; engineers second. Mode: Experience with Persuade's clarity duty.
Constraints: PRODUCT.md, owner taste (B/W, quiet, no cursor-chasing objects), sound effects with a visible toggle, iPhone/WebKit.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every site is drawn as a sheet in a construction drawing set, and a section line, the blade, cuts the finished surface open to show the structure underneath, measured from the real page. It refuses the screenshot-card grid and the greeting hero.

OWN-WORLD: Drafting paper and ink with graphite greys; a dark section field where structure draws in white hairlines; ruled title blocks; dimension lines and leader callouts in a technical mono used only for measurements; one display grotesk shown both solid and as outlines with bezier nodes; section markers (circle, letter, arrow A–A).

STORY: The visitor reads the name and role, sees the blade cut through it, learns there are 18 live sites, drags or scrolls the section line across each sheet to see how it was built, reads short notes and honest labels, then contacts or downloads the CV from the closing title block.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The name across the full width, cut by a diagonal section line A–A: above, solid ink letters on paper; below, the same letters as outlines with nodes and handles on the dark field. Navigation on top; bottom-left the intro (full-stack developer, BINUS cyber security, 18 live sites) with See the work and Download CV; bottom-right the title block (sheet, date, status open to roles).

FORM: Construction drawing set, position 1 of my grounded list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK; the roll assigned position 6), seed f8113a54.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the draggable section line with a hilt handle; dragging scrapes steel in proportion to speed and releases with a short ring; on each project sheet the section line moves with scroll and can be dragged to compare surface and structure.
