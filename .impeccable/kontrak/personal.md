# Surface brief: /personal/ (comparison round, variant E)

Scope: the whole portfolio as one page, English, for HR recruiters screening a full-stack developer; engineers second. Mode: Experience with Persuade's clarity duty.
Owner pins (2026-11-09, after sharing michaelkimeison.com as a reference): he likes the big personal photo in the hero and the light, airy, friendly feel; he chose "Versi E: rasanya, bukan tiruan". Use the LinkedIn portrait for now (shown no larger than it stays sharp). Do NOT reproduce the reference's (Dennis Snellenberg-derived) signatures: no multilingual greeting loader, no centred cut-out with a name marquee, no "Based in" pill, no circle "Talk with me" button, no placeholder testimonials. Constraints: PRODUCT.md, sound with a visible toggle, iPhone/WebKit.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Visiting his studio: the person in front of the work he has shipped. A friendly, light page where the portrait and the eighteen live sites share the first screen, so a recruiter meets the person and the proof at once. It refuses both the faceless grid portfolio and the copied centred-portrait template.

OWN-WORLD: Cool light grey room with white boards and near-black ink; soft rounded panels with real depth; one friendly sans (Albert Sans) in heavy display and light reading weights; the portrait as a colour cut-out; site captures shown as quiet greyscale prints that bloom into colour when you engage; a single small green dot reserved for "live".

STORY: The visitor sees his face, name and role, learns in one sentence what he builds and that 18 sites are live, checks the strongest projects in large friendly rows, browses all 18 with honest labels, sees what he works with, reads his background, and ends at a warm contact block with email, WhatsApp and CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: the name in two heavy lines, a first-person sentence, See my work and Download CV, and a short facts line (Jakarta, BINUS, intern, open to roles). Right: a tall rounded studio panel; behind the portrait two columns of his site captures drift slowly upward in greyscale; the cut-out portrait stands on the panel's bottom edge; a chip on the panel reads the live count after a real check.

FORM: Owner-pinned direction E, built after the seed-f8113a54 round (reference qualities, own composition).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the studio panel: hovering or touching it pauses the drift and blooms the captures into colour; each capture is a link to its site, and the live chip counts up as hosts answer.
